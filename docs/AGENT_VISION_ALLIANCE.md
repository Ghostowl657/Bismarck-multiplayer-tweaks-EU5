# Vision alliance

Adds a new consensual mutual relation, `bismarck_vision_alliance`, inspired by the spectator use case in Gekkaversalis Rebalance Campaign v3.2 (Workshop 3791919953). This is a new implementation, not imported upstream code; no upstream baseline was invented.

Both countries must be human, non-rebel, distinct, not allied, and not enemies. `can_create_subject_alliance` supplies vanilla subject-diplomacy restrictions. Opinion, rivals, coalition membership, religion, hegemon status, and unrelated wars are not eligibility conditions. Offers require consent; cancellation is immediate and permitted during shared wars. The pact grants visibility only, consumes no diplomatic capacity, and grants no access, basing, map-sharing permission, favors, institution spread, or calls to war. Becoming enemies, becoming a subject, becoming ordinary allies, or losing eligibility ends it. Human disconnect/reconnect handling requires real multiplayer validation.

Files: `in_game/common/scripted_relations/bismarck_vision_alliance.txt`, `in_game/common/scripted_triggers/bismarck_vision_triggers.txt`, `main_menu/localization/english/bismarck_vision_l_english.yml`.
